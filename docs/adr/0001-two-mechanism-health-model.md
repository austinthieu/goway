# Two-mechanism health model: active health checks + circuit breaker

A Backend's eligibility for traffic could have been driven by a single failure signal (one counter, one flag). Instead we use two independent mechanisms — a slow active `/healthz` prober and a fast per-Backend Circuit Breaker reacting to live-traffic Failures — and a Backend is Eligible only when both agree. We chose this over one shared signal because the two failure modes need different reaction speeds: the breaker must trip within a handful of requests to stop hammering a backend that's up but broken, while the active checker is the only signal that can independently confirm recovery once traffic has stopped flowing to a broken backend. Considered and rejected: a single passive failure counter feeding the same `Healthy` flag the active checker writes — simpler, but conflates "confirmed down by direct probe" with "suspected down from traffic," and removes the fast-trip/half-open-recovery behavior that's central to what a circuit breaker demonstrates.
